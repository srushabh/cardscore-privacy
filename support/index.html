An offline scorekeeper for card games, board games and friendly competitions.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. CardScore works without an account or sign-in.

Does CardScore need the internet?

No. Everything works offline, and the app makes no network connections.

Does CardScore sync between devices?

No. Your games, players and statistics are stored only on the device you use.

How do I start a game quickly?

Tap Quick start on the Home screen and choose Individual or Teams. You can rename players and teams on the scoreboard with a long press.

Can teams score together or per player?

Yes. When you set up a team game, choose Team total (one score per team each round) or Individual scores (one score per member, combined by Sum or Average).

I entered a wrong score. Can I fix it?

Yes. Open History on the scoreboard, tap the round and choose Edit scores, or tap Undo at the top of the scoreboard.

How do I share results?

Open a finished game's results and tap the share button to share an image, a PDF or text.

How do I export my history?

Open Games → History → Export CSV, or Settings → Export all games (CSV).

How do I delete a game?

In Games or in Home's recent games, open the game's menu and choose Delete. You can also delete a finished game from the menu on its results screen.

What happens to my data if I uninstall the app?

Uninstalling CardScore or clearing its app data may remove your locally stored games. Device backups follow your operating-system settings. See the privacy policy for details.
