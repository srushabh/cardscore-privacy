1. Introduction

CardScore is an offline scorekeeping application designed to help users record and manage scores for individual and team games.

Your privacy is important to us. CardScore is designed to work without requiring an account or an internet connection. Game information is stored locally on your device, and the app does not operate a cloud service for collecting or storing your game data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information CardScore handles, how it is stored, and what choices you have.

2. Information Stored by CardScore

When you use CardScore, you may enter information such as:

  • Player names and team names.
  • Game names and game rules.
  • Round-by-round scores and results.
  • Game history and statistics.
  • Other information you choose to enter while using the app.

This information is stored locally on your device so that CardScore can provide its scorekeeping, history and statistics features.

You should avoid entering sensitive personal information into player names or other fields.

3. Information We Collect

We do not operate a server that collects your game information.

CardScore is designed without:

  • User registration or account creation.
  • Cloud-based game storage.
  • Advertising or advertising trackers.
  • Analytics or behavioural tracking.
  • Developer-operated collection of game scores or player information.

We do not receive your player names, scores, game history or statistics through CardScore's normal operation.

4. Internet Access

CardScore is designed to function offline.

The app does not require an internet connection for its core scorekeeping features. It does not transmit your game data to our servers.

Downloading the app, receiving updates and using the services of the app stores are separate activities governed by the respective platform providers' policies.

5. Sharing and Exporting

CardScore may allow you to export or share game results and information in formats such as PNG, PDF, text and CSV.

Sharing or exporting is initiated by you.

When you choose to share information, the operating system may display its share interface and transfer the information to an app or destination you select.

Once information is shared with another app, service or person, that recipient's own privacy policy and practices apply. We do not control how third parties handle information you choose to share with them.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your game information is stored in the app's local database on your device.

We do not maintain a separate online copy of your game data.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate security for your device, including its screen lock and operating-system security updates.

Device backups, if enabled through your device's operating-system settings, may handle app data according to the relevant platform provider's backup policies. We do not control those platform-level backup services.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your game information remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app and its associated data.

You can delete games and other supported information using the app's available controls.

Uninstalling CardScore or clearing its app data may remove locally stored information. Recovery may not be possible unless a suitable device backup exists.

We do not retain a separate server-side copy of your game information.

8. Children's Privacy

CardScore does not require users to provide personal information to create an account.

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated online service. If you have concerns about information entered into the app by a child, please contact us.

9. Third-Party Services

CardScore's core functionality does not depend on third-party analytics, advertising or cloud-storage services.

The platform providers through which you download or update CardScore, and any apps you choose to use when sharing exported information, operate independently and have their own privacy policies.

10. Your Privacy Choices

You can:

  • Choose what information to enter into CardScore.
  • Avoid entering sensitive personal information.
  • Delete games and other information using the app's available controls.
  • Choose whether to export or share information.
  • Manage device backups through your operating-system settings.

Because CardScore does not maintain a server-side account or database of your game information, we generally cannot retrieve, access or delete information stored only on your device on your behalf.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when CardScore's functionality or privacy practices change.

The latest version will be made available at the published Privacy Policy URL. The effective date at the top of this document indicates when the policy was last updated.
